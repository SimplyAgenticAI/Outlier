Sage — your AI analyst

Sage reads your captured posts, the per-group baselines, and how Tallgrass scores them, then answers questions and recommends what to write. Pick a provider and paste your own key.

The key is stored in this app's local database in plain text, and is sent only to the provider you choose. Remove it by deleting data/outlier.db. That file is gitignored, so it never reaches GitHub.

Your brand profile

Tell Sage who you are. It makes Sage advise in your voice for your audience, and steers the graphic generator toward your look. Every field is optional — the more you add, the more on-brand the output.

Sample data

Generated posts that demonstrate the interface. Safe to remove at any time — it never touches anything you captured.

Danger zone

Deletes every post, source, saved item and remix. Use this when captures were recorded with an older extension — posts carrying zeroed engagement distort every baseline they sit in, and re-capturing has to start clean.
